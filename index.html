How the technical signals work

Golden Star (Custom): price, SMA20 and SMA50 converge while price and moving-average slopes strengthen. This is a transparent approximation, not StockInvest.us's proprietary formula.

Golden / Death Cross: SMA50 crosses above / below SMA200.

Bollinger Breakout / Breakdown: close crosses above the upper or below the lower 20-day, 2-standard-deviation Bollinger Band.

MACD Cross: MACD(12,26) crosses its 9-period signal line.

Unusual Volume: session volume exceeds your selected multiple of the prior 20-session average.

52-Week Breakout: closing price exceeds the prior 252-session high.

Volume-Confirmed Breakout: price closes above the prior 20-session high while volume exceeds your selected multiple of the prior 20-session average.

Gap: opening price versus the prior session close.

Best Day to Buy: compares weekday average/median prices and how often each weekday produced the lowest intraday price of its calendar week.

Codex Score: a transparent 0–100 research score combining profitability, price trend, momentum, dividend, proximity to highs, volume quality, and bullish/bearish signal balance. It is a ranking aid, not a forecast.
