How signals are calculated

Gap Up / Gap Down: today's open compared with the prior trading day's close. A signal is recorded when the absolute gap reaches your threshold during the selected scan window.

Golden Star (Custom): this tool does not copy StockInvest.us's proprietary implementation. It flags a transparent candidate when Close, SMA20, and SMA50 converge inside your tolerance and price + moving-average slopes are strengthening. The exact rule is documented in scanner.js.

Beta: covariance(stock daily returns, SPY daily returns) ÷ variance(SPY daily returns), using the selected lookback.
